# tunnel — digging a tunnel under a river (Short 02)

Voice first; word times in `tunnel_words.json`, scene in `WeMadeIt_Unity/Assets/Scripts/TunnelScene.cs`.
Script shape and look follow `research/format.md` "What the 29M video does": a chain of problems in the
second person, one idea per frame, one calm earth, dark tunnel with warm lamps.

## Narration

1. How do you build a tunnel right under a river?
2. If you just dig, the roof gives way and the river pours in.
3. So you dig inside a steel tube, a shield that holds the ground up.
4. But digging by hand takes years, so you bolt a giant spinning wheel on the front.
5. This is a boring machine.
6. But wet mud pushes through the wheel and floods it, so you pack the space behind the wheel with dirt and squeeze back.
7. Now the river can't get in.
8. But the dirt has nowhere to go, so you run a giant screw through the wall.
9. But the hole behind you starts to collapse, so a robot arm lines it with concrete rings.
10. But water seeps through the joints, so every joint gets a rubber seal.
11. And how do you move forward?
12. You shove against the ring you just built.
13. Congratulations. You have built a tunnel under a river.
