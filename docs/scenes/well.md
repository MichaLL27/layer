## Narration (v2: spoken hook first, Lazar 2026-11-01: say what the video is about from the first second)

1. How is a well dug, and why is its water clean? Let's cut the ground open.
2. Under your feet, water hides in sand, gravel and cracked rock. A well is how we reach it.
3. First, a drill rig bores a narrow hole straight down, sometimes hundreds of feet.
4. But a bare hole would cave in. So a strong pipe, called the casing, is lowered inside to hold the walls.
5. Now comes the part that keeps your water clean. The gap around that pipe is filled with clay and cement, so dirty rain can't sneak down the outside.
6. At the bottom sits a screen, a pipe full of tiny slots. Water squeezes in. Sand stays out.
7. A pump drops down on a pipe and pushes the water up to your home.
8. On top, a sealed cap, and the ground slopes away so puddles never reach it.
9. Last, the well is flushed with chlorine, so the very first glass is germ-free.

Title options (draft): `How a Well Gets Dug (and Stays Clean) 💧` · `The Hidden Seal That Keeps Well Water Clean 💧`
