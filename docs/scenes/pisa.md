# pisa: saving the Leaning Tower (Short 05, idea, no reference)

Voice first; word times in `pisa_words.json`. Script shape: the problem chain of `research/format.md`.

Facts (checked 2026-11-09, Wikipedia and geological-digressions.com; Ingenia refused the fetch): about 14,500 tonnes;
3 m deep foundation in weak, soft ground (clay, silt and sand, water table 1-2 m down); lean began during building
(1178); 5.5 degrees by 1990, closed that year. 1993: 600 t of lead on the north (high) side, later about 900 t.
1995: anchors planned about 40 m down; liquid nitrogen froze the ground in 178 holes to keep water out of the digging,
and on a September night the tower moved south, more than in the whole year before. 1999-2001: soil drawn out from
under the raised north side by holes drilled at 20-30 degrees (12 in the trial, 41 in the full job), 38 m3 / 70 t in all;
the top moved back 45 cm (17.5 in). Stable for at least 200 years (sources say 200 to 300; the lower one is used).

## Narration

1. This tower is about to fall over, and you have to save it.
2. It's nearly fifteen thousand tons of marble on a foundation ten feet deep, in soft, wet clay.
3. And one side has been sinking for eight hundred years.
4. So you stack six hundred tons of lead on the high side, and it stops.
5. But it's ugly, so you try steel cables, anchored deep underground.
6. To dig for them, you freeze the wet ground.
7. And overnight, the tower lurches the wrong way.
8. So you pile on three hundred more tons, fast.
9. Now you do the opposite of what makes sense.
10. You leave the sinking side alone, and you dig under the side that isn't sinking.
11. A drill slides in at an angle and pulls out the soil, a little at a time.
12. The hole closes, the high side settles, and the whole tower rolls back.
13. Seventy tons of dirt, and the top moved back eighteen inches.
14. It still leans, on purpose, and it's safe for at least two hundred more years.

## Scene idea

Hook: looking up at the whole tower as it starts to go. Then tower and ground are sliced in half like a cake: marble
above, solid masonry in section, foundation, sand, grey clay. North = left, south = right. The lean stays at its true
5.5 degrees; the half-degree the tower came back is shown at the top against a marker of where it was.
