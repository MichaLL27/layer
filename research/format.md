## What is observed (from 10 frames per video, 1 frame / 7 s)
- **Title pattern:** "How a <Thing A> Becomes a <Thing B>". Same house and backyard reused
  across three videos (series asset library).
- **Camera:** vertical, side-on cutaway: grass line near the top third, soil cross-section
  below with rock-texture, water layer in blue when relevant. Camera pans/zooms between the
  house, the pit and details; it is not static.
- **Captions:** ONE word at a time, centred, white with dark outline, rounded font, mid-screen.
- **Overlays:** simple arrows (white / orange / blue) showing force or flow; yellow numeric
  callouts ("= 15 CARS"); small icons. Minimal text beyond the caption.
- **Pacing:** a new visual state about every 2-4 s; the object is built/excavated in steps
  (dig -> reinforce -> pour concrete -> waterproof -> fit-out).
- **Characters:** one simple human figure (vest / skater) for scale, a few props.
- **Payoff:** last ~10 s shows the finished interior with details.

## What we copy (format, not content)
Cutaway view, word-by-word captions, one mechanism per video, step-by-step build, arrows and
one number callout, ~60 s, vertical.

## What must differ (REMAKE RULE)
Our house, character, palette, soil style, caption font/colour/position, camera moves,
duration (not 69-71 s: aim 50-55 s or 85-90 s), beat order, and topics' telling.

## To measure next
Word count per video (speaking rate), cut/camera-change timestamps, music/SFX style. Needs
the audio track: transcribe with a local speech-to-text and log it here.

## What the 29M video does (7J2V-np5Bks, You Made It, 29.2M views read 2026-11-03; `ref_7J2V/transcript.txt`)
- **Script = a chain of problems, in the second person.** "If you dig a hole..., you have a hideout, BUT soldiers spot it, SO you cover it. This is a trap door, BUT bombs cave the walls in, SO you dig deeper..." Every fix causes the next problem, so there is never a place to swipe away. Each fix gets a name ("This is an air vent"). It ends "Congratulations! You have built the Cu Chi Tunnels": the name comes last.
- **Picture = one idea per frame.** Most of the frame is one calm, fine-grained soil; the only detail is the thing being explained. Earth colours, natural light, no bright colours.
- **Dark tunnels with warm lamps** against the sunlit cut face. Simple graphics only: white arrows, a peach outline, one yellow number. Plain white captions, 60 fps.
- **People for scale and story** (soldiers, a villager carrying dirt), a dense forest behind.
- **It opens CALM** (a jungle floor), so a dramatic frame 0 is not what made this one work; the first sentence is.
